## About Me

I'm an AI engineer based in Bangladesh, currently working as an **AI Engineer Intern at ACI AI Business**. My work spans computer-vision datasets, model training and evaluation, large-scale inference, systematic error analysis, and synthetic-data pipelines.

I graduated in Mechanical Engineering from **Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET)**. That background shapes how I approach AI: as an engineering system that needs measurable behavior, reliable components, and careful validation.

- Building with Python across computer vision, LLM applications, and tabular or time-series ML
- Developing agentic workflows, retrieval systems, and vector-search pipelines
- Designing reproducible experiments with clear baselines and honest holdout evaluation
- Turning trained models into validated APIs and command-line tools
- Paying close attention to dataset quality, uncertainty, failure modes, and documentation
- Exploring AI systems that are useful in local and real-world contexts
